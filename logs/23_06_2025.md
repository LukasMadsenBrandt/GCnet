# 🧪 Status Log – Consensus Clustering at 0.004 Threshold  
**Date:** June 23, 2025  
**Author:** Lukas

---

## Summary

Since last Monday (June 16), two parallel consensus clustering analyses at the **0.004 GC threshold** have been running:

- One started from **50 partitions**, incrementally increasing 20% each run
- One started at **5000 partitions**, incrementally increasing 20% each run

Both analyses target the exploration of new genes .csv, which is a GC network (22,141 genes), with the goal of achieving stable community assignment using the Louvain algorithm, according to our standard stopping criteria.

---

## Current Status

### 1. **Run Starting at 50 Partitions**
- Initial partitions: 50
- Currently at: *over 1556* partitions (and still running)
- **No stable community detected** as measured by:
  - 90% quantile of relative differences
  - Top-5% gene overlap (threshold = 95%)

**Key log output:**  
- 90% Quantile of Relative Differences remains well above threshold (0.05), fluctuating between 0.11 and 0.17  
- Top 5% Genes Overlap consistently high (>95%) except for one outlier  
- No clear trend toward convergence



---

### 2. **Run Starting at 5000 Partitions**
- Initial partitions: 5000
- 6000 partitions completed after about a week
- Currently running: **7200 partitions**

**Key log output:**  
- 90% Quantile of Relative Differences: **~0.167** (threshold = 0.05)
- Top 5% Genes Overlap: **99.73%** (threshold = 95%)
- Overlapping Genes: **1104/1107**

No significant reduction in the 90% quantile, even with much higher partition numbers.

## Conclusions

- **Stability has not been reached** according to our stopping criteria (especially the relative difference threshold).
- The main computational bottleneck remains the size of the co-association matrix and the slow convergence of the relative difference metric.

---

## Next Steps / Recommendations

- Consider if alternative or adaptive stopping criteria would be acceptable for very large networks.
- Optionally, prioritize the 0.0004 network (where convergence was attainable and networks are interpretable) for downstream analyses and manuscript figures.
