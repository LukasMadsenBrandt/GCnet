**Author:** Lukas

---

## 🔍 Overview

Following our discussion, we've taken a step forward in refining the Granger Causality (GC) network analysis by comparing networks built using two distinct p-value thresholds:

1. `p < 0.004` (0.5% quantile)
2. `p < 0.0004` (0.05% quantile)

The goal is to evaluate how network sparsity and potential core structure shift under more stringent criteria.
